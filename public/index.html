Data security

Your payroll is sensitive. We treat it that way.

Penny runs on Propono's SOC 2 Type II certified platform. An independent auditor tested how we protect data over months, not on a single day. Here is what that means for you.

Encrypted end to end

In transit and at rest. Social Security numbers in uploaded records are redacted where they are not needed.

Used only for your audit

Your records are never sold or shared with other businesses. The free tools don't keep what you type.

Walled off by carrier

Each insurer's data is isolated. Only learned correction patterns are shared, never payroll, premium or names.

Logged and reproducible

Every result records its inputs and the rule and model versions, so it can be checked by you, an auditor or a regulator.
