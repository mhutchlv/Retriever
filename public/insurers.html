Grounded in research

How does an AI system prove which audit decisions it can make on its own?

That question is the subject of our research proposal to the U.S. National Science Foundation's SBIR program. Instead of asking you to trust an accuracy percentage, Penny is designed to guarantee a ceiling on dollar-weighted premium error for any audit type it automates, certified on your own corrections.

How it works

From policy expiration to final premium, in one system.

  1. 01Penny collectsTalks to your insured by chat, email, text or phone, loops in their bookkeeper or payroll provider, and keeps asking until the file is complete.
  2. 02Penny pre-buildsProposed class splits, payroll and state allocation, and flags on anything unclear, each with a confidence level and the rule behind it.
  3. 03Your auditor decidesReview, correct or send back. Auditors can ask Penny about any class code or rule and get an answer with citations.
  4. 04Penny handles questionsInsureds see every line explained. Disputes Penny can't settle go to your auditor, already organized.
  5. 05Every correction teachesAuditor decisions become training data and calibration data for your book, so the next audit starts closer to right.

Upgrade options

Reach insureds where they actually answer.

Most audit delays come from waiting on the insured. Add voice and text so Penny can follow up on their schedule, not yours.

Penny Text

Document requests and reminders by text.

  • Requests specific documents and sends a secure upload link
  • Accepts photos of payroll reports and certificates
  • Answers simple questions and escalates the rest
  • Reminds insureds before deadlines, so audits do not stall
Hi, this is Penny for your workers' comp audit. I still need your Q3 941. Upload it here: [secure link] Can I just send a photo? Yes. A clear photo of each page works. I'll check it and tell you if anything is missing.
Penny Voice

A patient phone call, any time of day.

  • Calls insureds to explain the audit and what's needed
  • Answers inbound calls 24/7 in plain language
  • Walks owners and bookkeepers through uploads step by step
  • Logs every call with a summary for your auditor
Why is my bill higher than my estimate? Your payroll for roofing came in above the policy estimate. I can walk you through each line, and if anything looks wrong, I'll send it to your auditor. Okay, walk me through it.

Voice and text are add-ons to any insurer plan, priced by usage. Insureds can opt out of calls and texts at any time.
