Privacy policy

What Penny collects, and what it doesn't.

This policy covers the Penny website and its free tools while Penny is in preview. It is written to match what the software actually does.

Last updated October 8, 2026 · Preview

Penny is in preview

Penny is a preview of a product from Propono Systems Inc. Please do not enter real payroll records, Social Security numbers, policy documents or anyone else's personal information. The free tools work with the numbers you type, and none of them needs personal details.

Who we are

Penny is operated by Propono Systems Inc. ("Propono," "we"). The site and its data are hosted on Microsoft Azure in the United States.

What we collect

  • Free tools. We do not keep what you type into a free tool. For each result we keep only a fingerprint: hashes of the input and output, the rule and engine versions used, and the time. You hold the full result as a receipt, and the fingerprint lets us confirm a receipt is genuine when you press Verify.
  • Chat. Your messages are used to answer you and are not stored. Chat currently runs on Penny's built-in answers and does not send your messages to any AI provider. If that changes, this page and the chat will say so before it happens.
  • Sign-up form. If you ask for early access or to talk to the team, we collect the name, email, role, interest and optional company you enter, only after you tick the consent box. Penny's chat never asks for contact details; only the form does.
  • Technical data. Our servers see your IP address. We hold it in memory for a short time to limit abuse (for example, too many messages a minute). Our hosting provider may keep standard request logs for security and operations.

Cookies and tracking

Penny does not use advertising or analytics cookies or tracking pixels, and does not sell or share your information for advertising. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google.

How we use it

  • To answer your questions and run the tools you ask for.
  • To contact you about Penny if you signed up and gave consent.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run Penny: Microsoft Azure for hosting and storage, Google Fonts for typefaces, and, when set up, a Microsoft Teams or Microsoft workflow notification that tells our team about a new sign-up. They may use the data only to provide their service to us. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Sign-ups: until you ask us to remove them, or until the preview ends, whichever comes first.
  • Free tool fingerprints: as long as needed to verify receipts. They contain no inputs or results.
  • Abuse-prevention data: minutes, in memory only.

Your choices and rights

You can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or withdraw your consent to be contacted at any time. California residents and residents of other states with privacy laws have these rights under their state's law. To make a request, contact the team through the form in Penny's chat and choose "Just talk to the team." We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Signed-in accounts later

When Penny opens accounts for auditors, businesses and partners, those accounts will store audit records under separate data terms that say exactly what is kept, for how long and who can see it. Each carrier's data will be walled off from every other carrier's. This policy will be updated before any of that starts.

Children

Penny is for businesses and professionals. It is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when what Penny collects changes, and change the date above. Questions: contact the team through the form in Penny's chat.

Penny by Propono Penny gives a neutral first review and does not replace your carrier's audit. Results depend on the documents provided and the rules in effect for your policy period.
